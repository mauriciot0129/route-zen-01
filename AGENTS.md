- When downsampling a photo with `createImageBitmap`, pass only `resizeWidth` (or compute both sides from the intrinsic size) — passing both `resizeWidth` and `resizeHeight` squeezes the picture out of proportion and makes labels harder to read.
- Reading a picture from the phone must never decode it at full resolution: downsample before decoding or drawing, because large photos have crashed the browser on the user's phone.
